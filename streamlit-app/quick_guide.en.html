Sunday Service Slides Editor · One-Page Guide

Edit each Sunday's worship content in the browser and build & download the whole-service PowerPoint (.pptx) in one click. No PowerPoint install, no local server.

🚀 Quick start (6 steps)

  1. Open the app URL and enter the password to log in.
  2. Pick the service date on the left; if the week is missing, use 「＋ 新增週次」 and enter YYYY.MM.DD.
  3. On the 「✏️ 編輯內容」 (Edit content) tab, fill the sections with the nine step buttons at the top (any order works).
  4. Click 「💾 儲存目前內容」 (Save current content) on the left.
  5. Click 「📽 製成整場投影片（合併＋編譯）」 (Generate full service).
  6. Click 「⬇️ 下載 PPTX」 (Download) to get Sunday_Service_<date>.pptx.
The nine sections: 1 Call to Worship · 2 Hymns · 3 Offering · 4 Scripture · 5 Sermon · 6 Response Hymn · 7 Communion · 8 Announcements · 9 Announcements MP4

🗂 The two tabs

  • ✏️ 編輯內容 (Edit content): a guided wizard, one section at a time; choose the service-wide font at the top.
  • 📽 製作成投影片 (Build slides): check each section's source (upload / cloud / web content), then build and download the service — and use 「🔍 檢查整場投影片」 to verify the deck.

💾 Save reminder

  • While you type, edits live in the browser session only — save (💾 儲存目前內容) before switching dates or logging back in.
  • Week content lives in Google Sheets (falls back to a local file); fetching scripture from fhl.net auto-saves once.

⭐ Main features

  • Scripture fetch & auto-pagination: pick a book from the dropdown and type a reference (e.g. 詩篇 34:1-3), then fetch from fhl.net in one click; multi-book, cross-chapter, 和合本 / 和修版 / 新譯本; auto-splits by font size and never breaks mid-punctuation.
  • Hymns & Response Hymn: ① search the lyric library → ② pick a song from the 「結果」 results → ③ click 「⬇️ 匯入整首詩歌」 (import the whole hymn) to fill in the full lyric and title card automatically. 4 built-in hymnals (教會聖詩 · 粵語其他詩歌 · 美樂頌 · 輔助本), plus background images, text shadow, lyric size 48–52pt, and text colour (white + black shadow or black + white shadow for dark/light backgrounds). When done, use the 🎬 產生全部詩歌的投影片 button at the bottom of the section (or 🎶 產生詩歌回應的投影片 for the response hymn) to build and download the whole .pptx in one go.
  • Upload or fetch from SharePoint: any section accepts a ready-made .pptx (the file wins), or fetch standard names from SharePoint (psalm_ / sermon_ / announcements_…).
  • Communion / Apostles' Creed: tick to include; auto-ticked on the first Sunday of the month.
  • Announcements MP4: upload a video or build one in the app; it can be merged as page 1 of the service to loop until clicked.
  • 🔍 檢查整場投影片 (Check full-service deck): verifies the scripture ↔ sermon reference, hymn page counts (≥ 4 pages each), lyric/typo comparison against the hymn library, and the first-Sunday communion template.
  • Scripture-only flow: builds just scripture_<date>.pptx; not part of the full service and not saved to Sheets.

🎬 Watch the demos (highly recommended)

Sections that have a demo show a 「🎬 看操作示範」 (Watch demo) button next to the title; clicking it loops that section's real workflow in a pop-up (~3 s per frame). Watch first, then follow along — the fastest way to learn.
