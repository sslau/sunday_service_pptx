生命多奇妙，多美好；
我一切困惑，祂全知道；
我所獻給祂的是憂傷破碎心靈，
但祂賜給我美好的新生命。
Something beautiful, something good;
All my confusion He understood:
All I had to offer Him was 
brokenness and strife,
But He made something beautiful 
of my life.
