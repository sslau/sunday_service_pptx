=== Slide 1 ===
聖詩 357-1
我的平安
My Peace
=== Slide 2 ===
我將平安賜給你，這平安非世界所能給，
這平安非世界所能夠瞭解，
藉它知，靠它活，我將平安賜給你。
My peace I give unto you.
It’s a peace that the world can not give.
It’s a peace that world can not understand.
Peace to know, peace to live, my peace I give unto you.
