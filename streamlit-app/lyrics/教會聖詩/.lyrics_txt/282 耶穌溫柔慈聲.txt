=== Slide 5 ===
聖詩 282

耶穌溫柔慈聲
Softly and Tenderly
=== Slide 6 ===
耶穌溫柔慈聲懇切呼喚你，
祂呼喚你快歸家；
耶穌站在你心門外等候你，
等候你來迎接祂。

Softly and tenderly Jesus is calling,
Calling for you and me; 
Patient and loving, He’s waiting and watching, 
Watching for you and for me.
=== Slide 7 ===
歸家，歸家，
傷心愁悶者，歸家；
耶穌溫柔慈聲懇切呼喚你，
「歸家，懇請你歸家！」

Come home, come home, 
Ye who are weary, come home; 
Earnestly, tenderly, 
Jesus is calling- 
Calling, “O sinner, come home!”
=== Slide 8 ===
為何你還推卸耶穌的懇請？
祂懇請你今歸家；
為何你還耽延忽略主救恩？
祂因救你把血灑。

Why should we linger 
when Jesus is pleading, 
Pleading for you and for me?
Why should we wait, then, 
and heed not His mercies,  
Mercies for you and for me?.
