=== Slide 10 ===
聖徒齊來歡唱：	哈利路亞！阿們！
	讚美基督我王；	哈利路亞！阿們！
	大家一心頌揚，	在寶座前歡唱；
	主必喜悅欣賞：	哈利路亞！阿們！
Come, Christians, 
join to sing Alleluia! Amen!
Lord praise to Christ our King; 
Alleluia! Amen!
Let all, with heart and voice, 
Before His throne rejoice; 
Praise is His gracious choice: 
Alleluia! Amen!
=== Slide 11 ===
齊來向主仰望：哈利路亞！阿們！
	頌讚聲滿天上；哈利路亞！阿們！
	祂是牧者良友，降卑代贖罪尤；
	祂的慈愛永久：哈利路亞！阿們！
Come, lift your hearts on high; 
Alleluia! Amen!
Let praises fill the sky; 
Alleluia! Amen!
He is our Guide and Friend; 
To us He’ll condescend; 
His love shall never end: 
Alleluia! Amen!
=== Slide 12 ===
再來讚美基督：哈利路亞！阿們！
	生命喜樂滿足；哈利路亞！阿們！
	在天福樂美境，主前崇拜奉敬；
	歌唱永遠不停：	哈利路亞！阿們！
Praise yet our Christ again; 
Alleluia! Amen!
Life shall not end the strain; 
Alleluia! Amen!
On heaven’s blissful shore 
His goodness we’ll adore, 
Singing for evermore,
Alleluia! Amen!
