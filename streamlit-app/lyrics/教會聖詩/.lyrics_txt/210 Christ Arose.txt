Hymn 210
=== Slide 17 ===
主在墳墓安睡，耶穌，我救主！
等待復活日來，耶穌，我主！

Low in the grave He lay,
Jesus, my Savior!
Waiting the coming day,
Jesus, May Lord!
=== Slide 18 ===
兵丁守墓無用，耶穌，我救主！
封石也歸虛空，耶穌，我主！

Vainly they watched His bed,
Jesus, my Savior!
Vainly they sealed the dead, 
Jesus, my Lord!
=== Slide 19 ===
死權不能困主，耶穌，我救主！
因已被主得勝，耶穌，我主！

Death could not keep his prey,
Jesus, my Savior!
He tore the bars away,
Jesus, my Lord!
