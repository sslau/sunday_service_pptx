=== Slide 7 ===
1.	I wonder as I wander out under the sky,How Jesus, the Savior, did come for to die. For poor, ornery people like you and like I, I wonder as I wander, out under the sky. 我徘徊在日光之下希奇思想，主耶穌竟為我受死由天降，為你，為我，困苦罪人而心傷，我徘徊在日光下，希奇的思想。
