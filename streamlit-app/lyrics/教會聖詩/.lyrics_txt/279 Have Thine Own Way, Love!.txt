=== Slide 1 ===
聖詩 279

憑你意行，主
Have Thine Own Way, 
Lord!
=== Slide 2 ===
1.	憑你意行，主！憑你意行！
	主你是陶匠，我是泥土；
	陶我與造我，照主旨意，
	我在此等待，虔誠候主。

	Have Thine own way, 
	Lord! Have Thine own way!
	Thou art the potter, I am the clay!
	Mold me and make me
	After Thy will,While I am waiting,
	Yielded and still.
=== Slide 3 ===
2.	憑你意行，主！憑你意行！
	鑒察試驗我，就在今天；
	求主洗淨我，潔白如雪，
	我謙卑跪下，在主腳前。

	Have Thine own way, 
	Lord! Have Thine own way! 
	Search me and try me,
	Master,today! Whiter than snow, Lord, 
	Wash me just now,
	As in Thy presence humbly I bow.
=== Slide 4 ===
3.	憑你意行，主！憑你意行！
	憂傷與疲倦，求主幫助；
	能力與權柄，全屬我主，
	醫治與拯救，惟靠救主。

	Have Thine own way, 
	Lord have Thine own way!
	Wounded and weary, Help me, I pray!
	Power all power Surely is Thine!
	Thouch me and heal me, Savior divine!
=== Slide 5 ===
4.	憑你意行，主！憑你意行！
	管理我動作，憑主指引；
	聖靈充滿我，顯明主恩，
	惟求主耶穌，永住我心。

	Have Thine own way, 
	Lord! Have Thine own way!
	Hold o’er my being absolute sway!
	Fill with Thy Spirit ‘Til all shall see
	Christ only, always, Living in me!
