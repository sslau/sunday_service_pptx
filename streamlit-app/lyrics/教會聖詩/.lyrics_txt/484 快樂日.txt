O Happy Day
=== Slide 20 ===
前有一日，我意立定，信靠耶穌救我靈魂；那時心中實在高興！願到四方傳揚主名。

	O happy day, that fixed my choiceOn Thee, my Savior and my God!Well may this glowing heart rejoice,And tell its raptures all abroad.
