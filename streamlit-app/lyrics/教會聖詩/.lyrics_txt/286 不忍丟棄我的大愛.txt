聖詩 286

不忍丟棄我的大愛
O Love 
That Will Not Let Me Go
=== Slide 2 ===

For another day
When Your sovereign hand will
Turn the final page!
And all will be accomplished,

And those who've longed to see You

1.	不忍丟棄我的大愛，疲倦靈魂因你得安；
今將所受生命獻上，藉你無窮生命汪洋，
得以豐沛久長。
