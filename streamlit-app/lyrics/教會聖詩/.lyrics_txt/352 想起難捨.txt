想起難捨
 Lord Jesus, When I Think of Thee
=== Slide 2 ===
Now the world is waiting
For another day
When Your sovereign hand will
Turn the final page!
And all will be accomplished,
Our trials and tears will end
And those who've longed to see You
Will never wait again!
1. 主耶穌啊！想起了你，心中便覺甜蜜；
	巴不得今天就被提，與你同在一起。

	哦！你是園中的鳳仙花，你是沙崙的玫瑰花，
	你是谷中的百合花，使我不能捨下。
