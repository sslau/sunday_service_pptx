=== Slide 1 ===
聖詩  468

信徒奮興
O Zion, Haste,
Thy Mission High Fulfilling
=== Slide 2 ===
信徒奮興，快將福音去傳揚，
普告天下，唯有真神是光；

O Zion, haste, 
thy mission 	high fulfilling,
To tell to all the world 
that God is light;
=== Slide 3 ===
使人知道，真神不願人沉淪，
不願有一人，黑暗裡滅亡。

That He who made all nations is not willing 
One soul should perish,
lost in shades of night.
=== Slide 4 ===
傳揚好信息，和平福音，
傳揚主耶穌已經救贖罪人。

Publish glad tidings, tidings of peace,
Tidings of Jesus,	redemption, 
and release.
=== Slide 5 ===
快去傳講，向萬族萬國萬邦，
真神是愛，是世人的保障；

Proclaim to every people,
tongue, and nation 
That God in whom they live 
and move is love;
=== Slide 6 ===
為救罪人，虛己降生到人間，
釘死十架上，使人進天堂。

Tell how He stooped to save 
His lost creation, 
And died on earth that man 
might live above.
=== Slide 7 ===
傳揚好信息，和平福音，
傳揚主耶穌已經救贖罪人。

Publish glad tidings, tidings of peace,
Tidings of Jesus, redemption, 
and release.
=== Slide 8 ===
差傳子女，去傳榮耀的福音，
奉獻金錢，差他們到遠方；

Give of thy sons to bear the message glorious,
Give of thy wealth to speed 
them on their way;
=== Slide 9 ===
恆切代禱，幫助他們打勝仗，
今日你奉獻，來日必蒙賞。

Pour out thy soul for them in prayer victorious, 
And haste the coming of the glorious day.
=== Slide 10 ===
傳揚好信息，和平福音，
傳揚主耶穌已經救贖罪人。

Publish glad tidings, tidings 	of peace,
Tidings of Jesus, redemption,
and release.
