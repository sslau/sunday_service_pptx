=== Slide 6 ===
救主在禱告的園裡，他們捆綁主的手，
帶祂遊行備受唾罵；
They bound the hands of Jesus in the garden where He prayed; 
They led Him thro’ the streets in shame.
=== Slide 7 ===
聖潔無罪的救主，竟被他們吐唾沫，
說祂有罪要釘祂十架。
They spat upon the Saviour
So pure and free from sin;
They said, “Crucify Him; He’s to blame.”
=== Slide 8 ===
祂可求父差千萬天使，毀滅這世界，將祂釋放；
祂可求父差千萬天使，祂竟願受死，為你為我。
He could have called ten thousand angels 
To destroy the world and set Him free.
He could have called ten thousand angels,
But He died alone for you and me.
=== Slide 9 ===
在救主尊貴的頭上，被戴上荊棘冠冕，
戲笑祂說「看哪！這王；」
Upon His precious head 
they placed a crown of thorns;
They Laughed and said, “Behold the king.”
=== Slide 10 ===
被他們鞭打辱罵，又被嘲笑祂的名，
祂獨自忍受一切頂撞。
They struck Him and they cursed Him
And mocked His holy name.
All alone He suffered every thing.
=== Slide 11 ===
祂可求父差千萬天使，毀滅這世界，將祂釋放；
祂可求父差千萬天使，祂竟願受死，為你為我。
He could have called ten thousand angels 
To destroy the world and set Him free.
He could have called ten thousand angels,
But He died alone for you and me.
=== Slide 12 ===
祂向忿怒群眾忍辱，祂並未乞憐呼喊，
甘願背負羞辱十架；
To the howling mob He yielded;
He did not for mercy cry.
The Cross of shame He took alone.
=== Slide 13 ===
當祂大聲說「成了，」祂就捨了祂生命，
奇妙救贖計劃已完成。
And when He cried, “It’s finished,”
He gave himself to die;
Salvation’s wondrous plan was done.
