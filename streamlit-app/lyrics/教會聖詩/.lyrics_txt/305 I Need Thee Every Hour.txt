=== Slide 11 ===
Hymn 305

我時刻需要你
I Need Thee Every Hour
=== Slide 12 ===
1. 我時刻需要你，救主恩深；
誰有柔聲似你，足慰我心。
I need Thee every hour, 
Most gracious Lord;
No tender voice like Thine 
Can peace afford.
=== Slide 13 ===
《副歌》

哦，救主，我需要你；
每時刻需要你！
望主隨時施恩惠，我來就你。
I need Thee, O I need Thee;
Every hour I need Thee!
O bless me now, my Savior —
I come to Thee.
=== Slide 14 ===
2. 我時刻需要你，與我親近；
魔鬼若來試探，使我得勝。
I need Thee, O I need Thee;
Stay Thou near by;
Temptations lose their power
When Thou art nigh.
=== Slide 15 ===
《副歌》

望主隨時施恩惠，我來就你。
I need Thee, O I need Thee;
Every hour I need Thee!
O bless me now, my Savior —
I come to Thee.
=== Slide 16 ===
3. 我時刻需要你，或憂或樂；
主若不來同居，我就枉活。
I need Thee every hour,
In joy or pain;
Come quickly, and abide,
Or life is vain.
