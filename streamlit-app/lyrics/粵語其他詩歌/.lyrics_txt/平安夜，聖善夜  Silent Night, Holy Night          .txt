=== Slide 1 ===
會眾詩歌4

平安夜，聖善夜
Silent Night, Holy Night          
  
旁述:  Samuel Han 韓益南弟兄
            Yidian Ye      葉依典姊妹
=== Slide 2 ===
I just can't sing the song 
“Silent Night“  without thinking 
of snow!

我只要一唱「平安夜」，就會想到雪！
=== Slide 3 ===
First of all, snow kind of muffles 
sound,  so if  you're in snow, it really
 is quiet! 

第一個原因是雪花可以遮掉許多聲音，
所以如果你在雪中，真的很安靜！
=== Slide 4 ===
This song was also written in “snow 
season" in the Alps  where Father 
Joseph Mohr lived. Right before
 Christmas, the organ in the church 
he pastored had broken.

這首歌也是在莫約瑟神父居住的阿爾
卑斯山雪季寫成的。他牧養的那間
教會，風琴偏偏選在聖誕節前罷工了！
=== Slide 5 ===
So that his congregation would still
have special music for the Christmas 
Eve service,  he decided to write a song. 

為了讓會眾在聖誕夜燭光晚會中仍
然有特別的音樂可聽，他決定寫一首歌。
=== Slide 6 ===
His friend, Franz Gruber, wrote the 
music,  and with just a guitar, they
sang their new song for Christmas 1818. 

他的朋友葛魯勃為他譜曲，兩人就靠一把吉他，為1818年的聖誕唱了這首新歌。
=== Slide 7 ===
But there's a third reason that I 
think of snow when I sing "Silent 
Night.“ and it’s  found in the third verse. 
It describes Jesus  as "love's pure light“

但我每唱「平安夜」一定想到雪，
還有另一個原因，是在第三段歌詞
裡，它形容耶穌的「神子愛，光皎潔」，
=== Slide 8 ===
and that with His coming we now see
 "the  dawn of redeeming grace." 
Jesus came to bring us the light that 
would show the way back to God. 

而且因著祂的降生，我們如今看到「救贖
洪恩的黎明來到」。耶穌到世上，
帶來了光，引領我們回到神面前。
=== Slide 9 ===
What does that have to do with 
snow? Well, the way back to God
comes Through  the forgiveness of 
sins. If we  believe in Jesus,  the Bible
 says that God will forgive us. 

這跟雪有什麽關係呢？回到神面前的路
是要通過赦罪的。聖經說，我們如果
相信耶穌，神一定會饒恕我們。
=== Slide 10 ===
No matter what we've done, no matter
 how stained our hearts have become 
through the  disobedient and wrong 
things we've done, the Lord will forgive
 us and make us clean. 

不管我們做什麽事，不管我們所做的悖逆
錯誤的事如何玷污我們的心，只要相信耶
穌，真心悔改，神會原諒我們，將我們洗淨。
=== Slide 11 ===
“ If we confess our sins, he is faithful
 and just and will forgive us our sins
 and purify us from all unrighteousness.”
 (1 John 1:9).

「我們若認自己的罪，神是信實的，是
公義的，必要赦免我們的罪，洗淨我
們一切的不義。」(約翰一書1:9)
=== Slide 12 ===
In the Old Testament, the Prophet
 Isaiah  described this ultimate act of
 forgiveness in this way: 

舊約聖經裡，先知以賽亞是這樣形容
最極致的饒恕。
=== Slide 13 ===
“Come and let's think about this
 together,“  says the Lord. "Though
 your sins are like scarlet, they shall
 be as white as snow;  though they 
are red like crimson, they 
shall be as wool" (Isa. 1:18). 

以賽亞書1:18主說：「來吧，我們彼此理
論，你們的罪雖然像朱紅，必變成白雪；
雖紅如丹顏，必白如羊毛。」
=== Slide 14 ===
The value of snow can be
seen in its beauty and in God‘s 
creativity  that no two snowflakes 
are alike.

雪的可貴在它的美，也在神驚人的
創造力：每片雪花都長得不一樣！
=== Slide 15 ===
But the real treasure we find in 
Snow is the  picture it gives us of
 what God wants to do  in our lives
 through  forgiveness. 

但雪花真正寶貴的地方，在於它呈現了
神要透過饒恕在我們身上成就的事。
=== Slide 16 ===
In the Psalms, David prays 
"Wash me, and I shall be  whiter 
than snow.“

大衛王在詩篇裡的祈禱，「求你
洗滌我，我就比雪更白」。
=== Slide 17 ===
Imagine! Whiter than snow! 
That’s how  clean God can make us, 
according to the riches of His grace.

你能想像嗎？比雪更白！神出於祂的洪恩，
可以使我們白於雪。
=== Slide 18 ===
” In him we have redemption through 
his blood, the forgiveness of sins, in
 accordance  with the riches of 
God’s  grace.”  (Ephesians 1:7) 

「我們藉這愛子的血，
得蒙救贖，過犯得以赦免，乃是照祂
豐富的恩典。」(以弗所書1:7)
=== Slide 19 ===
Next time you're out in the snow, 
thank God that He sent us His greatest
 treasure- Jesus and that through Jesus 
He makes us  as white as snow! 

下回你在雪中，記得感謝神送給我們最大的寶藏 — 耶穌，並且透過耶穌使我們白如雪！
