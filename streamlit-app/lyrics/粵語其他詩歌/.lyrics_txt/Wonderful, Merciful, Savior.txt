"Wonderful, Merciful, Savior" 

by Selah


               Liu  Sisters
=== Slide 2 ===
1. 上主是我堅固保障，
    是我山寨和避難所；
    苦海汪洋主為救星，
    四無生門我仍有望。
    雖有兇惡仇敵，
    攻擊不留餘力，
    對我仇恨刺骨，
    常用狡猾引誘，
    但我救主將我保護。
Wonderful, merciful Savior
Precious Redeemer and Friend
Who would have thought that a Lamb
Could rescue the souls of men
Oh, You rescue the souls of men
