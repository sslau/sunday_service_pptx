=== Slide 1 ===
You Raise Me Up
with Be Still, My Soul
你激勵我暨我靈鎮靜
Quartet  四重唱
=== Slide 2 ===
Be still, my soul! the Lord is on your side; 
Bear patiently the cross of grief or pain.
Leave to your God to order and provide;
In every change He faithful will remain.
我靈鎮靜上主今在你旁，
憂痛十架你要忍耐擔當；
信靠天父為你安排主張，
萬變之中惟主信實永長。
=== Slide 3 ===
Be still, my soul!
The wave and winds still know,
 His voice who ruled them 
while He dwelt below.

我靈鎮靜， 風浪依舊聽遵，
救主當年， 所發吩咐聲音。
=== Slide 4 ===
When I am down and, 
oh my soul, so weary; When troubles come and 
my heart burdened be;
當我行在人生的幽谷，
噢！我的靈，感到多麼的疲憊； 當苦難臨到，我的心背負重擔；
=== Slide 5 ===
Then I am still and wait here in the silence Until You come and sit awhile with me.
然而，我會在這裡静默等候， 直到祢來，伴我稍坐片刻。
=== Slide 6 ===
You raise me up so I can stand on mountains. You raise me up to walk on stormy seas.
祢激勵我，使我能站在群山之巔； 祢激勵我，使我能渡過狂風怒海；
=== Slide 7 ===
I am strong when I am on Your shoulders. You raise me up to more than I can be.
當我靠在祢的肩頭，我就剛強壯膽； 祢激勵我，使我能超越自己。
=== Slide 8 ===
There is no life, no life without its hunger. Each restless heart beats so imperfectly.
每個人的一生，都要經歷苦難； 每一顆跳動的心都不完美；
=== Slide 9 ===
But when You come, 
and I am filled with wonder, Sometimes I think I glimpse eternity.
但當祢出現時，
我的生命便充滿驚奇， 有時，我認為自己瞥見永恆。
