=== Slide 1 ===
我神真偉大 How Great Is Our God
=== Slide 2 ===
尊貴榮耀君王，在全地之上，願萬民都喜樂，全地都喜樂，主你滿有榮光，黑暗都躲藏，萬民高聲唱，齊來高聲唱。 
The splendor of a King, clothed in majesty,Let all the earth rejoice, all the earth rejoice.He wraps Himself in light, and darkness tries to hide,and trembles at His voice, and trembles at His voice.
=== Slide 3 ===
我神，真偉大！歌頌你聖名，真偉大，全地都看見，我神，真偉大！ How great is our God, sing with me,how great is our God!
And all will see how great, how great is our God!
=== Slide 4 ===
昔在永在君王從今直到永遠，你從不曾改變，永存在天地間，你是三一真神，父子與聖靈，真神的羔羊，全能神羔羊。
 And age to age He stands, and time is in His hands,beginning and the End, beginning and the End.The Godhead three in one, Father Spirit Son,the Lion and the Lamb, the Lion and the Lamb.
