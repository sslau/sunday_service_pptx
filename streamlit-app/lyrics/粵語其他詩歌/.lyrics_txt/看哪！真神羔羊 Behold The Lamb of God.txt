=== Slide 4 ===
祂來使我自由，受死為你和我，
醫治安慰憂傷的人們。

He came to set us free,
to die for you and me,
To heal and comfort those in sorrow.
=== Slide 5 ===
何罪與祂相關，慈愛和平君王 ?
祂本無罪，卻遭受折磨和大痛苦，大痛苦。

What was He guilty of, 
this man of peace and love?
He had no sin, 
yet suffered torment and agony, agony.
