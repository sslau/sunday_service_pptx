=== Slide 1 ===
天使頌歌
Angel’s Carol
=== Slide 2 ===
你曾否聽見天使悅耳歌聲
回音何等甜美，是何等清亮？

Have you heard the sound of 
the angel voices
Ringing out so sweetly, ringing out so clear?
=== Slide 3 ===
你曾否看見那星照耀輝煌，
像是主誕生從神來的記號？

Have you seen the star shining out so brightly 
As a sign from God that Christ the Lord is here?
=== Slide 4 ===
你曾否聽見天使帶來消息，
是給長久等待卑微的牧人？

Have you heard the news 
that they bring from heaven
To the humble shepherds 
who have waited long?
=== Slide 5 ===
榮耀歸於至高真神！(x2)
聽眾天使歡唱喜樂歌。

Gloria in excelsis Deo! (2x)
Hear the angels sing their joyful song.
=== Slide 6 ===
祂帶來平安在那寂靜寒冬，
像那輕柔雪花飄於平安夜；

He is come in peace in the winter’s stillness,
Like a gentle snow fall in the gentle night;
=== Slide 7 ===
祂帶來喜樂像那清晨日光，
用光明燦爛來充滿全世界。

He is come in joy like the sun at morning,
Filling all the world with radiance and with light.
=== Slide 8 ===
祂帶來慈愛甘願成為人子；
我們見祂誕生在卑微馬槽：

He is come in love as the child of Mary; 
In a simple stable we have seen His birth:
=== Slide 9 ===
榮耀歸於至高真神！(x2)
聽眾天使歌唱平安頌

Gloria in excelsis Deo! (x2)
Hear the angels singing ‘Peace on earth’
=== Slide 10 ===
祂將新真光帶入黑暗世界，
就像明星照耀在高天之上；

He will bring new light to a world in darkness,
Like a bright star shining in the skies above;
=== Slide 11 ===
祂將新希望帶入期盼國度。
用純潔和愛作王統管萬有。

He will bring new hope to the waiting nations
When He comes to reign in purity and love.
=== Slide 12 ===
讓世界歡樂為著救主來臨；
讓穹蒼用快樂晨光來響應：

Let the earth rejoice at the Saviour’s coming;
Let the heavens answer with the joyful morn:
=== Slide 13 ===
榮耀歸於至高真神！(x2)
聽眾天使歡唱「主誕生」。(x2)

Gloria in excelsis Deo! (x2)
Hear the angels singing  ‘Christ is born.’ (x2)
