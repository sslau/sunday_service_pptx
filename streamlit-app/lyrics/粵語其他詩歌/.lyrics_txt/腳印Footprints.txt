=== Slide 1 ===
足印
Footprints
團契遊樂園 3
Lorraine Ko     Corey Ko
=== Slide 2 ===
1.憶當天的那段舊夢，祂仿似與我夜裡共行，沙灘中的每段道路，編出祢與我兩雙足印。
One night I dreamed I was walking along 
the beach with the Lord. 
Sometimes there were two sets of footprints.
=== Slide 3 ===
漫漫路無論多少崎嶇，都有基督緊握我手，遇寂寞但也不孤單，明白祢是共行。
My Lord held tight to my hands through the rugged passages,
I was not feeling lonely because of the footprints in the sand.
=== Slide 4 ===
回望我過去多少的失意，但祢每次每次也在旁，
是祢抹我淚痕，是祢揹我經過。
During the lowest times of my life
 you were always beside me,
You were the one who wiped my tears and carry me through.
=== Slide 5 ===
明白到有祢居於心深處，我不再是獨行，
前路有祢共行，留下雙雙足印。
I am understand I was not alone 
because you were in my heart,
I know you will walk with me all the way 
with footprints in the sand.
=== Slide 6 ===
2. 祂當天親身的應許，祂必會與我伴結前行，
	一生中的每段道路，都必會帶領我經過，
	The Lord promised me, that He’ll never leave me.
	He’ll continue to lead me alone the way.
=== Slide 7 ===
路漸盡回望我的一生，雖有祂一起的腳蹤，
	但絕望並困境之中，全是孤單足印。

It happened at the very lowest
 and saddest times in my life.

3. 每次我心中多悲傷，祂怎卻遠去剩我獨行；
	每次我感到是寂寞，祂怎卻遠去剩我孤單；
Lord, how could you leave me alone when I was feeling sad?
Lord, how could you leave me alone
 when I was feeling lonely?
=== Slide 11 ===
默默地在我身邊的主，跟我低聲輕輕說出；
「在絕望路裡的一雙，原是我的足印。」
The Lord replied quietly, 
“During your times of trial and suffering,
when you see only one set of footprints, 
it was then that I carried you.”
