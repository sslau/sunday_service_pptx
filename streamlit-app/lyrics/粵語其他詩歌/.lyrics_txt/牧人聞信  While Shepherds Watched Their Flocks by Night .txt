=== Slide 1 ===
會眾詩歌3：

牧人聞信
While Shepherds Watched 
Their Flocks by Night                      

旁述:  Yidian Ye      葉依典姊妹
Samuel Han 韓益南弟兄
=== Slide 2 ===
耶穌降生的宣告，居然是牧羊人
第一個聽見！這有誰能猜想得到
呢？牧羊人自己絕對想不到！

Who would have ever thought that it would be the shepherds who would receive the first announcement of Jesus' birth? Certainly not the shepherds!
=== Slide 3 ===
當時的牧羊人應該是相當粗野的。
他們的工作非常艱苦，不但大部
分時間生活在野外，還要和野獸
格鬥，保護羊群。

Shepherds back then would have belonged to a pretty rugged crowd. They had a very physically demanding job: they lived outside with the sheep for a large portion of the year, and they had to fight wild animals to protect their sheep.
=== Slide 4 ===
他們說話不太文雅、身上不怎麼
乾淨、舉止更不高雅，反正羊兒
們也不計較這些嘛！

They didn't use the most refined language, they weren't very clean, and didn't have the most elegant manners. After all-the sheep didn't care!
=== Slide 5 ===
牧羊人可能都很窮，但他們卻是
第一批知道神子降生的人，而且
是從天使口中聽到！

They were also probably very poor. Yet these were the men who were the first to hear about the birth of God's Son, and from angels no less!
=== Slide 6 ===
這些牧羊人不但第一個得到特權
來朝拜耶穌，也是第一批有榮幸
去報告這好消息的人。

The shepherds were also the first ones who had the privilege of coming to worship Jesus, and the first ones who had the honor of telling others about Him.
=== Slide 7 ===
所以，雖然牧羊人粗俗、口袋空
空如也、又沒什麼教養，但神揀
選了他們！

So, in spite of the fact that the shepherds were rough, uneducated, and poor men. God chose them!
=== Slide 8 ===
你有沒有想過，神有一天會選擇
如何用你？

Have you ever thought about how God might choose to use you someday?
=== Slide 9 ===
從牧羊人身上，我們學到，人不
須要特別聰明、超級富有、或是
衣冠楚楚、或是風度翩翩、或是
職場得意。

From the shepherds we can learn that you don't have to be super-smart, you don’t have to be super-rich, and you don't have to have fine clothes or great manners or a wonderful job.
=== Slide 10 ===
你根本不須要十全十美，只要像
牧羊人一樣，在神對你說話的時
候願意順服！

You don't even have to be perfect. You just have to be willing to obey when God speaks to you! Just like the shepherds.
=== Slide 11 ===
這首歌作者的晚年有點像這些
牧羊人 — 坎坷又貧困。

The man who wrote this song was a man kind of like the shepherds-rough and poor.
=== Slide 12 ===
他叫那鴻•泰特，一生高低起伏。
小時候很不錯，生在一個愛爾蘭
傳道人家裡，讀的是好學校，後
來成為有名的詩人。

His name was Nahum Tate and he had a rather sad life. It started out well enough in the home of a minister in Ireland. He went to good schools and became a famous poet.
=== Slide 13 ===
但那鴻過世前是個酒鬼，身無分文
，住在救濟院裡。

But by the time Nahum died he was an alcoholic, penniless, and living in a poorhouse.
=== Slide 14 ===
幸好他心裡知道有神，因此寫出
這首聖誕頌歌，說到神怎樣來到
人的景況中，與他們相遇。

Yet in Nahum's heart there was an awareness of God, and he wrote this Christmas carol about God meeting people where they are.
=== Slide 15 ===
也許那鴻比誰都明瞭 — 就是人的
一生不論如何失敗，神仍然來到他
面前與他相遇，吸引他歸向基督。

Maybe Nahum understood this better than anyone-that no matter how people may have failed in their lives, God still wants to meet them right where they are and draw them to His Son.
=== Slide 16 ===
神照你的本相與你相遇。不須要
等到你夠成熟了，神才和你說話
或使用你。

He wants to meet you where you are. You don't have to wait until you're grown up for God to talk to you or use you.
=== Slide 17 ===
「不可叫人小看你年輕；總要
在言語、行為、愛心、信心、
清潔上，都作信徒的榜樣。」
(提摩太前書4:12) 

“Don’t let anyone look down on you because you are young, but set an example for the believers in speech, in conduct, in love, in faith and in purity.” （1 Timothy 4:12)
=== Slide 18 ===
神現在就要用你！神可以使用任
何人，甚至用牧羊人、用那鴻、
用你！神總是要把屬祂的人帶到
祂所期待的最高境界，甚至從
他們小時候就開始！

He wants to do it now! God can use anyone-even shepherds, even Nahum, even you! He always wants to bring His people to the highest goal that He has for them-He can even start when they're kids!
=== Slide 19 ===
你只要仔細聽祂的聲音，並且
順服祂，祂就會使用你，就像
那些牧羊人一樣！

He will do that for you if you listen for His voice and obey Him... just like the shepherds!
