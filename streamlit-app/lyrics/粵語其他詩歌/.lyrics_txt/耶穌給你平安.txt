=== Slide 1 ===
耶穌給你平安Jesus Gives You Peace
=== Slide 2 ===
『這世界雖有苦難，
主耶穌是避風港灣；
祂要給你，祂要給你平安，
源源湧流不斷。』(2X)

“Though the world had suffering, 
Jesus is our safe harbor; 
He gives you, He gives you Peace,
Ever flowing without ceasing.” (2X)
=== Slide 3 ===
耶穌，耶穌給你平安。
耶穌給你真正平安。
深深在你心裡，源源湧流不斷；
哦！耶穌給你平安。

Jesus, Jesus gives you Peace.
Jesus gives you true Peace.
Deep down in your heart, 
	ever flowing without ceasing;
Jesus gives you Peace.
