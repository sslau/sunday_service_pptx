=== Slide 2 ===
在一馬槽，有一嬰孩，把那乾草作小床
由天而來，純潔聖嬰，為我們今天降生。

In a stable, lies a baby,
In a manger bed of hay;
Sent from heaven, infant holy,
Who is born for us today.

=== Slide 4 ===
看那母親與小聖嬰，發出光芒顯輝煌，
聽見天使歌聲悠揚，樂韻聲不斷迴響。

See the mother, with the Christ Child.
How they shine in radiant light. 
Hear the angels singing sweetly 
as they carol through the night.

=== Slide 6 ===
獻禮物，獻尊崇讚美，屈膝覲見主君王。
俯伏崇拜，高聲歡呼，與眾天使同歌唱！


Bring your gifts, bring honor and praise.
Kneel before the blessed King.
Bow in homage, your voices raise.
With the angels join to sing!
=== Slide 7 ===
主已說明藉此嬰孩，在卑微馬槽降生，
齊來尊崇，同心敬拜，因祂降臨拯救人。

God has spoken through this baby,
Who is born in lowly stall.
Let us worship and adore him,
Who has comes to save us all.
