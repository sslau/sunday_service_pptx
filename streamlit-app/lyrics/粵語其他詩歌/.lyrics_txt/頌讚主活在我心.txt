=== Slide 10 ===
躺於青草邊安歇祢旁，
In green pasture, lay down, 
find refuge next to you.
祢引領我經幽谷險風浪，
Valley shadow & tempest raging, 
you led me through.心底雖枯乾，主聲音導前望。
When my heart seemed withered, your words assured the way ahead.

 in my talking,細聽祢與我輕聲的禱求，

永遠讚美祢應當高歌獻奏。
You’re worthy to be praised,
 I offer my voice to sing.

give me peace.頌讚主活在我心，天天拖帶作導航，

cares for me.
