=== Slide 2 ===
Mandarin Choir
國語詩班
From the hillside of Golgotha,
Jesus is heard.
The echoes still linger,
Bringing His dying words.

來自山坡的各各他，
聽那主耶穌的聲音。
回音依然縈繞，帶來了主最後的話語，
=== Slide 3 ===
Mandarin Choir
國語詩班
All of time cannot silence
the words of that day.
The echoes still linger, 
ever here to stay.

那日的字句，歲月無法抹滅。
回音依然縈繞，永遠存留。

“Father, forgive them.
Father, forgive them all.
Father, it is finished.
it is finished.”


父啊！赦免他們！父啊！赦免他們！
父啊！成了！成了！
=== Slide 5 ===
Mandarin Choir
國語詩班
Hear the words of the angry crowd,
“Crucify!” they cry.
The echoes still linger,
hear His final sigh.


聽那憤怒的眾人喊叫，釘他十字架！
回音依然縈繞，聽祂最後的嘆息。
=== Slide 6 ===
Mandarin Choir
國語詩班
To remember the Savior
who died to set me free,
may the gift of forgiveness 
ever be with me.


要記念救主捨命使我得自由，
願赦罪的恩典永遠與我同在。
