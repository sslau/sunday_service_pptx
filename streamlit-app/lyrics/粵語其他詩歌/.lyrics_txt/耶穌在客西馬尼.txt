=== Slide 1 ===
耶穌在客西馬尼
Jesus, In Gethsemane
 
Douglas Nolan
J. Paul Williams
中文: 程亦妮
國語詩班
Mandarin Choir
=== Slide 2 ===
耶穌在客西馬尼，橄欖樹下求旨意，
俯伏屈膝花園裡，痛苦哀號聲悲淒。
 
Jesus, in Gethsemane, 
Praying neath the olive trees,
falling there on bended knee,
crying out in agony.
=== Slide 3 ===
我主，憂傷至極，充滿謙卑心意，
掏空心靈在暗夜裡。
 
	Our Lord, in deepest grief, 
filled with humility,
poured out His soul into the night.
=== Slide 4 ===
父啊，將這杯挪去，將這杯挪去，
	如此安靜之地，死亡圍繞進逼，
	祂將恩典的杯高舉。

	Father, “take this cup from me”(x2).
There, in that quiet place,
He felt death’s cold embrace 
and lifted high the cup of grace.
=== Slide 5 ===
父啊，將這杯挪去，將這杯挪去。
	父啊，父啊，父啊！

Father, take this cup from me
take this cup from me.
Father, Father, Father!
=== Slide 6 ===
耶穌在客西馬尼，面對自己的命運，
尊榮地位祂放棄，甘心選擇骷髏地。
 
Jesus, in Gethsemane,
 reaching for His destiny, 
lays aside His majesty, 
freely choosing Calvary.
=== Slide 7 ===
就在那花園牆內，當夜幕漸漸低垂，
神子為愛放棄一切。
 
	There, inside the garden wall,
as the night begins to fall, 
God’s Son surrenders all for love.
=== Slide 8 ===
父啊！願祢旨意成就，願祢旨意成就。
父啊！嗚！耶穌在客西馬尼！
耶穌在客西馬尼！客西馬尼！
 
	Father, let Thy will be done. 
Let Thy will be done. Father! Oo! 
Jesus, in Gethsemane. 
Jesus, in Gethsemane. in Gethsemane.
