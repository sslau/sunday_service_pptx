=== Slide 1 ===
頌讚主榮耀
I Will Sing Your Glory and Praise
國語詩班
=== Slide 2 ===
主，我生命滿你豐美，
一切遭遇顯主愛深廣。
當看見早晨光輝，渡過平安寂靜夜，
我知你必常與我同在。

Lord, You fill my life with beauty.
I can find Your love in all I see.
From the early morning light through 
the peaceful calm of night, 
I know You are always close to me.
=== Slide 3 ===
主，我生命滿你奇珍，
在我心靈滿有主平安，心靈有平安。
每當我迷失方向，陷在低谷困境中，
我知我永不孤單無助。

Lord, You fill my life with wonder.
I can fill Your peace deep within my soul.
As I try to find my way through the trials of the day, 
I know I will never be alone.
=== Slide 4 ===
我心甦醒，你樂歌振興心靈。
我靈高飛，歡呼主恩多奇妙。
將生命獻你，願被你使用，
顯揚你榮耀，一生讚美你。

My heart’s alive with the joy of Your music.
My spirit soars with Your wondrous gift of grace.
Please take my life, Lord, and help me use it to sing Your glory, and to sing Your praise.
=== Slide 5 ===
主，你賜我生命真義，
我作一切，必有主參與。
我願尋求你心意，立志遵守你命令，
我知我能時刻信靠你。

Lord, You fill my life with meaning.
I can feel Your touch in all I do.
As I try to understand how to live as You command, 
I know I can always trust in You.
=== Slide 6 ===
我心甦醒，你樂歌振興心靈。
我靈高飛，歡呼主恩多奇妙。
將生命獻你，願被你使用，
顯揚你榮耀，頌主榮耀，榮耀，
我要頌主榮耀！

My heart’s alive with the joy of Your music.
My spirit soars with Your wondrous gift of grace.
Please take my life, Lord, and help me use it 
to sing Your glory, sing Your glory. 
Glory, I will sing Your glory!
=== Slide 7 ===
歡欣，奮興，忠實真誠，
向主獻歌頌揚神，你賜厚恩，
衷心感謝，我要頌主榮耀永感恩，
榮耀！頌讚！


Ever joyful, ever faithful, 
Lord, to You my song I raise.
With a heart forever grateful,
I will sing Your glory and Your praise,
glory and praise!
