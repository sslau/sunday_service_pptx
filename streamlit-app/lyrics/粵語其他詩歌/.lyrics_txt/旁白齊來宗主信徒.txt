齊來，宗主信徒
O Come, All Ye Faithful 

旁述:   Yidian Ye      葉依典姊妹
Samuel Han 韓益南弟兄
=== Slide 2 ===
聖誕季節有好多好多活動可以
參加：在教堂有特別的聚會，燭
光聖誕夜崇拜是我的最愛！

Christmastime is filled with lots of things to go to: there are special church services, (Candlelighting service is my favorite!)
=== Slide 3 ===
班級同樂會、詩班獻詩、
聖誕樹裝飾聚會、報佳音、
聖誕劇、家人吃團圓飯；
還有...還有... 嗯...party! 派對！同樂會 ！


class parties, choir recitals, tree-trimming
parties, caroling parties, Christmas plays, 
family dinners, and… well... parties, parties, parties!
=== Slide 4 ===
這些活動你都是從哪裡知道的
呢？ 當然是因為接到邀請啦！
所以除了收到聖誕卡和聖誕禮物，
你通常還會接到邀請。


How do you find out about all of these occasions? You receive an invitation, of course! So, along with receiving Christmas cards and Christmas presents, you usually receive some invitations too.
=== Slide 5 ===
但不可或缺的只有「聖誕的兩
項邀請」 ，有了這兩項，耶穌
才能在你家過聖誕。接下來這
首詩歌「齊來宗主信徒」，
兩項都提到了。

But the only invitations that really matter are the invitations that make it possible for Jesus to spend Christmas at your house, and they are both talked about in the song, “O Come. All Ye Faithful.”
=== Slide 6 ===
第一項是神給我們的邀請，祂
說 :「凡信我的都到我這裡來」 。
耶穌到世上本來就是因為神邀
請我們到祂那裡。約翰福音3:16
說: 神將祂的兒子賜給我們。

The first invitation is the one God makes to us in the words. “O come, all ye faithful." The fact that Jesus came to earth in the first place was an invitation by God to come to Him. John 3:16 says that God gave His Son.
=== Slide 7 ===
我們只須要信，也就是接受祂的
邀請！在聖經裡，我們一遍又一
遍地看到耶穌召我們來：祂邀請
我們到祂那裡去、同祂去歇一歇、

All we have to do is believe-or accept the invitation He gives! In the Bible we see Jesus over and over again calling us to come: He invites us to come to Him, to come and rest,
=== Slide 8 ===
讓小孩子到祂那裡去、
來跟從祂、來得生命，
還有來和祂一同坐席。

to let children come to Him, to come and follow Him, to come and receive life, and to come and eat with Him.
=== Slide 9 ===
祂要我們在祂身邊！但我們也
要願意親近祂。聖經還說耶穌
站在我們的心門外問我們： 
「你願意開門讓我進去嗎？ 」

He wants to have us near Him! … But we have to want the same thing. The Bible also says that Jesus is standing at the door of our hearts asking. “Will you open the door and let Me come in?"
=== Slide 10 ===
祂就是在等待你打開心門！
你若開門，耶穌應許「要進
到你那裡去」。

All He's waiting for is for you to open the door of your heart! If you do, He promises, "I will come in.“
=== Slide 11 ===
「看哪！我站在門外叩門；若有
聽見我聲音就開門的，我要進到
他那裡去，我與他，他與我一同
坐席」 。(啟示錄3:20 )

“Here I am! I stand at the door and knock. If anyone hears my voice and opens the door, I will come in and eat with that person, and they with me. “
(Revelation 3:20)
=== Slide 12 ===
在「齊來虔誠同崇拜」這句歌
詞裡，可以看到第二項邀請。


The second invitation can be found in the words, 
"O come, let us adore Him." We make this invitation to the Lord through our worship and praise.
=== Slide 13 ===
聖經說我們的頌讚確實是在
邀請神，到我們所在的地方
來！(詩22:3)

The Bible says that our praise actually invites 
the Lord to come and be where we are!
(Ps. 22:3)
=== Slide 14 ===
聖經也說，雖然我們從前因罪與
神隔絕，但如今藉著接受耶穌的
邀請，我們可以「親近神」。

The Bible also tells us that though we were once separated from God by our sins, now, through accepting Jesus' invitation, we can "draw near to God."
=== Slide 15 ===
「並我們心中天良的虧欠已經
灑去，身體用清水洗淨了，就
當存著誠心和充足的信心，來
到神面前。」(希伯來書10:22)

Let us draw near to God with a sincere heart and with the full assurance that faith brings, having our hearts sprinkled to cleanse us from a guilty conscience and having our bodies washed with pure water. 
(Hebrews 10:22)
=== Slide 16 ===
想想看，我們接受耶穌救贖的邀
請，神就接受我們敬拜的邀請。

Just think about this: accepting Jesus' invitation of salvation makes it possible for God to accept our invitation of worship!
=== Slide 17 ===
我們可以親近神，祂也可以
親近我們，親愛的程度，就像
我們和自己的爸爸媽媽一樣。

We can draw near to God and He can draw near to us with the same love and closeness you have with your mom or dad.
=== Slide 18 ===
你已經接受耶穌的邀請了嗎？
如果還沒有，為什麽不在今天
就接受呢？打開你的心門讓祂
進來。

Have you accepted Jesus‘ invitation? If you haven’t, why don‘t you do that today? Open the door of your heart and let Him in.
=== Slide 19 ===
如果你已經接受了，今天就來
敬拜祂，邀請祂進入你家。
你看！耶穌要和你一同過聖誕！

If you have done that, why don't you worship Him today, inviting Him to come to your house. You see, Jesus wants to spend Christmas with you!
