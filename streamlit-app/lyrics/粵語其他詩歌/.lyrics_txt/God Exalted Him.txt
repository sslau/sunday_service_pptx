=== Slide 1 ===
God Exalted Him
神將祂高舉
Tom Fettke
Linda Lee Johnson
國語詩班
     Mandarin Choir
=== Slide 2 ===
He became a man; Christ became a man.
And laid aside the glory He had known.
祂成為了人，基督成為了人，
捨棄榮耀，離開天上寶座，
=== Slide 3 ===
To be a carpenter, a simple carpenter.
A lowly servant, rejected by His own.
甘願為木匠，卑微作工匠，
降世為僕人，被自己子民棄絕。
=== Slide 4 ===
But God exalted Him. God has uplifted Him,
And has given Him a Name above all Names.
但神將祂高舉，神已將祂升為至高，
賜祂超乎萬名之上的名，
=== Slide 5 ===
That every knee should bow 
in earth and heav’n above.
And every tongue confess that Jesus Christ is Lord.
使天上、地上萬膝都要跪拜，
萬口承認耶穌基督是主！
=== Slide 6 ===
He became a man; Christ became a man.
Whose destiny was death upon a tree.
祂成為了人，基督成為了人，
順服至死，釘死於十架之上，
=== Slide 7 ===
Still He became a man, a gentle, loving man,
A man of sorrow, acquainted with grief.
仍然成為人，溫柔慈愛之人，
憂傷痛苦，滿心憐憫憂患。
