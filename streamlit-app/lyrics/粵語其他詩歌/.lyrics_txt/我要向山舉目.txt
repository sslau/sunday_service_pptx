=== Slide 1 ===
I Lift Up My Eyes 
Unto The Hills
我要向山舉目
=== Slide 2 ===
I lift up my eyes unto the hills,
From whence does my help comes?
My help comes from the Lord above,
Who earth and heaven has made.
我要向山舉目,我的幫助從何而來？
我的幫助是從造天地的耶和華而來。
=== Slide 3 ===
He will not let your foot ever slip ;
He who keeps you will not slumber nor sleep.
The Lord is your keeper and your shade upon
your right hand.
祂必不叫你的腳搖動,保護你的必不打盹。
保護你的是耶和華 ,耶和華在你的右邊蔭庇你。
=== Slide 4 ===
The sun will not smite you nor will the moon;
your days and your nights will be safe.
The Lord will protect you from all evil;
He will keep your soul.
白日太陽必不傷你,夜間月亮必不害你。
耶和華要保護你,免受一切的災害。
祂要保護你的性命。
=== Slide 5 ===
I lift up my eyes unto the hills,

=== Slide 6 ===
Glory to the Father, and to the Son,
and to the Holy Ghost.As it was and as it is now,
and ever always shall be. 
榮耀歸於聖父、聖子、聖靈,
從今時直到永遠。
