=== Slide 1 ===
無十字架就無冠冕

Without His Cross
=== Slide 2 ===
無祂眼淚就沒有安慰，
無祂的死就無生命，
無祂寶血就沒有赦罪，
無十字架就無冠冕﹔

Without his tears there is no comfort. 
Without His death, there is no life. 
Without His blood there is no pardon. 
Without His cross, there is no crown.
=== Slide 3 ===
無祂羞辱就沒有榮耀，
無祂嘆息就無喜樂，
無祂鞭傷就沒有醫治，
無十字架就無冠冕。

Without His shame, there is no glory. 
Without His sigh, there is no joy. 
Without His wounds,there is no healing. 
Without His cross, there is no crown.
=== Slide 4 ===
親愛主你施行拯救，
用慈愛蓋上印記，
用你的大愛除去我悲傷，
你的恩典大顯現﹔

Dear Lord you give salvation, 
It's sealed by your mercy. 
Your great love takes away my grief. 
Your grace has manifested.
