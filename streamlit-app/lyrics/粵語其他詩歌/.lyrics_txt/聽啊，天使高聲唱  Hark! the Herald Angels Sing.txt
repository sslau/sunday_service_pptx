=== Slide 2 ===
Charles Wesley wrote "Hark, the 
Herald Angels Sing" during a time
 when very few Christmas songs 
were being written.

在查爾斯•衛斯理寫「聽啊，天使高聲唱」的時代，很少有人創作聖誕詩歌。
=== Slide 3 ===
About 100 years earlier, the Puritans
 had held such political power in 
England that they had Christmas
 carols abolished because they thought 
they were part of a worldly celebration. 

在那之前大約一百年，清教徒在英國的政治勢力大到足以廢止聖誕歌曲，因為他們認為這些歌曲流於靡靡之音。
=== Slide 4 ===
The Puritans thought that if the
 holiday  wasn't going to be only about
 Jesus then nothing should be celebrated. 

清教徒認為，如果節慶不是單單為耶穌而設，那就沒什麽好慶祝的。
=== Slide 5 ===
In a way, they were concerned about
 the same things we are today. When 
we look around, there doesn't seem to 
be very many people emphasizing Jesus
 at Christmas. 

說起來，他們當初擔心的事跟我們今天是差不多的。我們看看周圍，好像很少人把耶穌當作聖誕節的主角。
=== Slide 6 ===
Most people put the emphasis on 
Santa Claus and getting presents. But
 although leaving Jesus out of Christmas
 is a sad thing, it does give us an opportunity to make Christmas a testimony about God's love for all people.

大多數人的焦點放在聖誕老公公和聖誕禮物。但是雖然聖誕節不紀念耶穌是很可悲的事，這節日卻給我們機會見證神對所有人的愛。
=== Slide 7 ===
One of the best ways to do that is to
let people see the joy Jesus brings 
into our lives! 

有一個很棒的方法，就是叫人看見耶穌給我們的生命帶來多少喜樂!
=== Slide 8 ===
God doesn't just want us to be holy
 (set apart for Him). He wants us to 
be happy while we're being holy!

神不僅要我們成為聖潔(就是歸耶和華為聖的意思)，祂也要我們既聖潔又喜樂！
=== Slide 9 ===
“But let all who take refuge in you be
 glad; let them ever sing for joy.
Spread your protection over them,  
that those who love your name may 
rejoice in you.” （Psalm 5:11)  

「凡投靠你的，願他們喜樂，時常歡呼，因為你護庇他們。又願那愛你名的人，都靠你歡欣。」(詩篇5:11)
=== Slide 10 ===
One way we can share God's holiness
 and happiness is through music! Look 
at what Charles did with his song: 

透過音樂我們可以分享神的聖潔和歡樂！
看看衛斯理透過這首歌分享了什麽？
=== Slide 11 ===
In verse 1 he simply invites us to join
 the angels in praise for the gift of God’s 
Son and what it accomplished-"God 
and sinners reconciled!" That means 
that we've been brought back into a
relationship with God!
 
第一段裡他直接邀請我們和天使一同頌揚神子這大禮，加上祂所成就的「神和罪 人能和好！」意思是我們已經與神重建關係。
=== Slide 12 ===
But in verses 2 and 3, he blended
 together so many different Scriptures,
 that people can see all of God's plan. 
The song by itself is a testimony of God’s 
love! Look at all of these Scripture verses! 

第二段和第三段的歌詞融合了很多經文，使我們能看到神完整的計劃。這首歌本身就見證了神的愛！來看看底下這些經節。

=== Slide 21 ===
As we celebrate this Christmas, let’s
 show Jesus' love to those around us:
 through our music, through our 
celebration, through our commitment
 to Jesus, and through our joy! 

今年慶祝聖誕的時候，讓我們透過我們的音樂、透過我們的慶祝、透過我們向基督的委身、更透過我們的歡欣喜樂，向周圍的人展現耶穌的愛。
