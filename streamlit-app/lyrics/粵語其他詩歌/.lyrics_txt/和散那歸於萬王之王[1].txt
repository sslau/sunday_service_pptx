=== Slide 1 ===
和散那歸於萬王之王
Hosanna to the King of Kings
國語詩班
Mandarin Choir
=== Slide 2 ===
Hosanna to the King of Kings!        
Lift your voice in praise and sing:                
He comes this day, O glorious day!              
Let your loud Hosannas ring!
和散那歸於萬王之王!
揚聲讚美歌唱：  
祂來臨這日是何等榮耀輝煌！
你要高聲唱和散那！
=== Slide 3 ===
Let the people shout, 
let the rocks cry out!            
He comes in the name of the Lord! 
Hosanna to the King of kings! 
Sing Hosanna to the King!
讓眾民歡呼，讓石頭呼叫。
祂是奉主名來的，
和散那歸於萬王之王!
和散那歸我王。
=== Slide 4 ===
Lift up your heads, O mighty gates; 
Open wide, you ancient gates, 
Open wide that the King of glory may come in!    
Let us worship and adore.
眾城門哪，你們要抬起頭來；
敞開你永久的門户，
敞開，讓榮耀的王進來！  
讓我們俯伏敬拜。
=== Slide 5 ===
Who is this King of glory?  
Who comes in the name of the Lord?    
He is the Lord Almighty! 
Sing Hosanna to the King!
榮耀的王是誰呢？
誰是奉主名來的？
祂是全能的王！
唱和散那歸基督我王。
=== Slide 6 ===
Hosanna, hosanna! 
Sing praise to Christ, the King!
We sing hosanna, hosanna!
Blessed is the one who comes today 
in the name of the Lord!
和散那，和散那！
稱頌基督君王，
我們唱和散那，和散那！
賜福的神今日來到，
這奉主名來的是應當被稱頌！
=== Slide 7 ===
Hosanna to the King of king!
Wave your palms joyfully sing!
See the King who comes on a donkey’s colt,
Let your loud Hosannas ring!
歡唱和散那歸於萬王之王!
揚起棕樹枝迎接，
祂騎着小驢駒來到，
唱和散那歸萬王之王。
=== Slide 8 ===
Rejoice,  O people of Zion!
He comes to set you free…
Hosanna to the King of kings!
Hosanna to the King! 
Sing hosanna to the King!
歡欣,錫安的民哪！
祂來拯救你從罪中得釋放…
和散那歸萬王之王!
和散那歸王!唱和散那歸王！
